Queensland's libraries: where they are and who they reach

More than 300 libraries serve Queensland, from Brisbane's city branches to small outback branches. Do people use them equally everywhere?

Borrowing is far from even

In 2024-25 the average Queenslander borrowed 7.7 items from their council library. Gold Coast residents borrowed 12.3 each, while some remote councils borrowed fewer than one. Grey councils have no published figures.

Busy doors, big and small

Visits tell a different story from loans. Brisbane recorded 5.4 million visits and the Gold Coast 2 million, but libraries are also places to use computers, meet people and get help. Circles sit at the centre of each council.

Who borrows the most?

The highest-borrowing councils are mostly in the south-east. Smaller rural councils are spread widely, and some borrow as much as city councils.

What libraries offer

Early childhood programs are about 31% of all sessions but draw about 51% of everyone who attends, so storytimes and rhyme sessions reach the most people.

Back to the library, after COVID

Across Australia, in-person visits fell during the pandemic and have recovered to about 3.2 per person. Borrowing has grown from 5.5 to 6.5 items per person, while website visits have dropped.
